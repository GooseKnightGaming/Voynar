The Voynar Folios

These pages are written in Voynar, a script of 62 glyphs. Every folio carries a real message. Nobody outside its author knows how to read it. Can you?

The folios

Switch any folio to its transcription to work with plain letters instead of glyphs. Each glyph has one transcription letter, shown in the chart below.

The first folio has not been released yet. Check back soon.

The glyphs

The transcription alphabet: each glyph and the letter used for it in the transcription files. The letters are labels only. They say nothing about what a glyph means.

The challenge

  1. Everything you are given is on this page: the folios, their transcriptions and the glyph chart.
  2. How the script works is not published. Working that out is part of the challenge.
  3. Think you have read a folio? Open an issue on the GitHub repository with the folio number and the first five words of your reading. Please don't post a full solution publicly.
Voynar was created by GooseKnightGaming, inspired by the Voynich Manuscript (Beinecke MS 408).
